💧 Waterbodies ▾

Chinnakurchi village, located in the Kengeri Hobli area of Bengaluru South, sits at an elevation of approximately 878 meters to 890 meters (2,880 feet to 2,920 feet) above mean sea level.

Over the last century, the waterbodies in the city have been built over - they have reduced in size with most of them disappearing completely. This should be prevented in the extending Bengaluru.

Categories of waterbodies identified in the village through GIS mapping and BMICAPA maps:

  1. Lake: seasonal, water bodies used for domestic activities and recharging rainwater. Often between 5-10 acres.
  2. Tank: Natural or Man-made tanks larger than 3 acres. Historically built for irrigation.

We have 3 functioning water bodies in the Chinnakuruchi village:

  • Waterbody 1 - receiving a catchment of 760 Acres
  • Waterbody 2 - receiving a catchment of 180 Acres
  • Waterbody 3 - receiving a catchment of 48 Acres
